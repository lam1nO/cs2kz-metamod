# CKZ crouch takeoff

The crouched vertical takeoff bonus requires duck to have been held on the ground in the preceding movement tick. Substeps within the same tick and duck carried over from the air alone do not qualify. Preserve forced crouch, crouched hull and air-duck behavior, ordinary ground-prepared crouch jumps, NOPRE's separate height policy and map-provided jump physics. Check the 57 and 66 unit thresholds when changing this path.
